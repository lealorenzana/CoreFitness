Background
There is increasing demand for health and wellness services in Mamburao, Occidental Mindoro, with their fitness centers (G-Fitness, Fitness Regency and Ferrer Fitness) still relying on manual and paper-based processes. Administering these facilities is inefficient, as they are based on handwritten training logs, physical membership folders and analog record-keeping mechanisms, which makes it difficult to provide services and maintain the business. As shown in the related studies, manual records of small and medium fitness facilities are becoming less and less effective due to various operational gaps, which is why digitalization is becoming more and more necessary (Zhou, 2022; Alboneh & Snae, 2025).

Interviews with stakeholders in the three gyms showed that manual methods are associated with a tendency towards the ‘data decay’ cycle, where paper-based records are piling up and are either thrown away or recycled on a regular basis, such as every couple of weeks. This practice leaves nearly all historical member data intact, thereby allowing owners to not be able to see member progress or trends in financial gaps. In particular, owners found that they could not access unpaid balances from previous periods, because no system was in place to identify outstanding balances. Without automated access control, unregistered individuals can enter the facility unnoticed during busy periods when staff attention is divided, leading to uncollected fees. Previous research has validated the efficiency of QR code-based attendance systems in enhancing accuracy and administrative productivity in service-oriented settings (Siew et al., 2023c), and the NLP-based automated chatbot systems in automating repetitive administrative questions and boosting service responsiveness (Ahriz et al., 2024).

The researchers used a purposive sampling method to select 60 respondents from the 3 collaborating fitness centers (G-Fitness with 40 respondents, Fitness Regency with 10 respondents, Ferrer Fitness with 10 respondents) to validate these operational inefficiencies and determine the readiness of the stakeholders in using a digital solution. To ensure that data was collected from people who were actively involved in the day-to-day functioning of the gym and physical training activities, purposive sampling was used. The survey was divided into four sections: current gym process, problems and challenges faced, willingness to contribute data to the development of the system, and perceived importance of proposed features of the system. Overall, the weighted mean for Section B (Current Gym Processes and Transactions) was 4.41 (Strongly Agree) which shows that the respondents recognize the existence of the gym processes and the manual and undigitized ways of conducting transactions in the gym. In the operational problems domain Section C (Problems and Challenges) the weighted means were 2.32 (Rarely) at all three gyms, indicating that operational issues are common to all three gyms, such as: delays in membership registration, gym information not available outside of gym hours, the lack of individual fitness advice and the difficulties in keeping track of attendance. Weighted mean scores for Section D (Data Gathering for System Development) were 4.28 (Very Willing) which depicts strong stakeholder receptiveness towards sharing of personal fitness and payment data in order to develop system. The weighted mean for Section E (System Needs and features) was 3.85 (Important), indicating that the target users agree that the features that are proposed, including 24/7 access online, mobile-optimized systems, digital attendance tracking, real-time notifications and an NLP based administrative assistant, are important. Put together, these results confirm the gaps outlined from stakeholder interviews and direct field observation, and provide the empirical evidence that can support the development of the CORE Fitness system.

Given these documented challenges, this study suggests the development of a cross-platform Management Information System (MIS) dubbed CORE Fitness as the digital backbone of local fitness centers. The architecture is designed to be scalable and flexible, using a relational database to store and manage data.The system is built on a Node.js and MySQL 8.0 architecture, with a focus on scalability and flexibility to accommodate the needs of a growing user base. Administered via a React based web dashboard, and a corresponding mobile app for members, operations are managed and functions of the gym can be accessed at any time without the need for physical records.

The system has two features built for the local environment: Rule-Based Analytics for keeping members, and an NLP-Based Administrative Assistant for handling common questions.  The analytics feature tracks attendance to identify members who may be at risk of being lost and can be used as a Decision Support System (DSS) that allows owners to take a proactive approach to retention strategies. The research on fitness center member behavior has shown that, to maintain fitness center level membership renewal, at-risk members need to be identified through behavioral data monitoring early on in the member's experience (Yeomans et al., 2024). The NLP Administrative Assistant responds to repetitive requests about gym policies, schedules and membership verification, a function that is especially desired by owners to ease communications and alleviate administrative conflict. CORE Fitness shows that an appropriately localized technology can provide the same data-driven operational benefits to small provincial businesses as those enjoyed by larger urban businesses, when assessed against ISO/IEC 25010 quality standards.

Problem Statement
The study aims to design, develop, and evaluate CORE Fitness, a cross-platform Management Information System with Rule-based Monitoring Analytics and NLP-based Administrative Support for Local Fitness Centers in Mamburao, Occidental Mindoro. The system focuses on improving gym operations through automation, centralized management, attendance monitoring, and digital administrative support to enhance operational efficiency and business sustainability. 
Specifically the study aims to address the following problems:
Local gyms in Mamburao experience difficulties in managing member records, attendance monitoring, and payment tracking due to manual and paper-based processes, resulting in inefficiency, data inconsistency, and delays in gym operations.
Gym owners and staff have limited capability to monitor member attendance trends and identify inactive or irregular members due to the absence of digital attendance monitoring and centralized tracking systems.
Gym members have limited access to digital services such as mobile-based membership management, attendance tracking, digital identification, account monitoring, and automated inquiries regarding gym services and announcements.
Existing gym management practices lack an integrated and centralized cross-platform system that provides real-time monitoring tools for membership management, financial performance, attendance tracking, staff scheduling, class management, secure web and mobile access, real-time notifications, QR code-based attendance monitoring, and centralized data management for both administrators and members. | Problem Statement

General Objectives
This study aims to design, develop, and evaluate Core Fitness, a cross-platform Management Information System with Rule-based Analytics and NLP-Based Administrative Support for Local Fitness Centers in Mamburao, Occidental Mindoro, that improves gym operations through automated attendance tracking, centralized management, rule-based monitoring, and digital administrative support.

Specific Objectives
1. To develop a Cross-Platform Fitness Ecosystem with the following features:
For Gym Management (Web-based Administration):
Centralized Operational Core: A core centralized system for managing membership records, QR code-based attendance monitoring, and payment reporting automation, replacing paper-based attendance and manual record-keeping.
Rule-Based Monitoring and Analytics Dashboard: A monitoring module that applies predefined system rules to monitor attendance records, membership renewals, inactive, and operational insights to support gym management and decision-making.
Business Intelligence Dashboard: A real-time analytics and reporting dashboard for monitoring membership growth, financial performance, attendance records, and facility utilization.
Staff and Schedule Management: A module for managing gym staff, class schedules, and workflow coordination.
Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): A multi-role access control mechanism supporting Administrator, Trainer, and Member roles with distinct permissions and system privileges.
Membership Registration and Approval Workflow: A registration process where members may register and submit membership applications, which require administrator approval and payment verification prior to account activation.
For Members(Mobile Application Experience):
NLP-Based Administrative Assistant (Chatbot): A chatbot designed to respond to common inquiries regarding gym schedules, membership information, announcements, and gym policies using Natural Language Processing (NLP).
Digital Identification and Access: A mobile interface with unique QR code for gym entry and secure access to membership profiles.
Personal Progress Monitoring: A comprehensive mobile dashboard that enables members to monitor attendance history, membership status, body measurements(weight, BMI, arms, waist, chest, and legs), attendance consistency, membership progress, goal achievement records, achievement badges, and visual analytics related to their gym activities.
Event and Announcement Management: A feature that allows administrators to publish gym announcements, schedules, events, and important updates accessible to members through the mobile application.
Account Management: A platform that enables members to manage their personal information, membership details, attendance records, and fitness-related activities within the gym system.
Trainer Rating and Evaluation System: A feature that allows members to submit trainer ratings, monthly evaluations, and feedback comments. The system generates trainer performance insights to support service quality improvement and member satisfaction.
Goal Achievement and Gamification System: Module that enables members to set fitness goals, monitor milestones, receive achievement badges, and track goal completion progress to encourage continuous gym participation.
For Trainers (Mobile Application - Trainer Role)
Trainer Dashboard and Class Management: A dedicated trainer interface for viewing assigned classes, monitoring schedules, managing training sessions, and reviewing member participation records.
Trainer Feedback and Recommendations: A feature that enables trainers to provide workout recommendations, performance evaluations, improvement suggestions, and assigned workout plans while monitoring member progress.
Booking and Availability Management: A module that allows trainers to manage availability schedules, review bookings, and accept or decline training session requests.
2. To build the system architecture using:
React.js: For the web-based administration dashboard;
React Native: For the cross-platform (Android/iOS) member mobile application;
Node.js & Express: For the back-end API and server-side processing;
MySQL 8.0: For centralized database management and secure storage of membership records, attendance logs, payment records, operational reports, and system transactions. 
Firebase: For user authentication and real-time notification services.
3. To test the application using the ISO 25010 software quality standards;
Functional Suitability;
Reliability;
Performance Efficiency;
Usability;
Security;
Compatibility;
Maintainability; and
Portability
4. To assess performance of the system in terms of enhancing the efficiency of the gym and the satisfaction of the users using the system via user acceptance testing. | General Objectives

Beneficiaries
Gym Owners and Administrators. Gym owners such as G-Fitness, Fitness Regency, and Ferrer Fitness will benefit from a more organized and modern way of managing their gyms. Instead of using paper logs and manual records, they will use a centralized digital system that stores all information in one place. They can easily manage member registration, attendance records, payments and membership status. The system also helps them view reports and summaries of gym operations, making it easier to monitor income and overall performance. With this, gym owners can save time, reduce errors, and make faster and better decisions for their business.
Gym Members. Gym members will benefit from a mobile application where they can easily access their personal account anytime. They can view their membership details, attendance history, and payment records without needing to ask staff. The system also allows them to track their gym visits, helping them become more aware of their attendance and consistency. This improves their experience because everything is accessible in one platform, making gym services more convenient and user-friendly.
Gym Trainers and Staff. Trainers and staff will have an easier and more efficient workflow because the system reduces the need for manual record keeping. They can quickly access member information, attendance logs, and class schedules through the system. This helps them monitor who is attending sessions and manage training schedules more effectively. It also improves coordination between staff and administrators since information is updated in real time. As a result, trainers can focus more on guiding members and improving training quality instead of doing paperwork.

Scope
The study aims to design, develop, and functionally validate CORE Fitness, a cross-platform Management Information System (MIS) with Rule-Based Analytics and NLP-Based Administrative Support for local fitness centers in Mamburao, Occidental Mindoro. The proposed system includes a web-based administration dashboard developed using React.js and a mobile application for gym members developed using React Native, integrated through Node.js and MySQL 8.0. The system focuses on automating gym operations such as QR code-based attendance monitoring, membership management, payment tracking, attendance recording, account monitoring, rule-based monitoring, analytics, and operational reporting. These processes are traditionally managed through manual and paper-based methods. The system also includes member-focused features such as personal progress monitoring, body measurement tracking (e.g., weight, BMI, arms, waist, chest, and legs), attendance consistency monitoring, membership progress monitoring, goal achievement tracking, achievement badges, trainer feedback viewing, and trainer evaluation.The system provides trainer-oriented functionalities including workout recommendations, performance feedback, workout plan assignment, class management, booking management, and availability scheduling. In addition, the system integrates an NLP-Based Administrative Assistant, or chatbot, designed to handle common administrative inquiries related to gym schedules, membership information, announcements, facility policies, account concerns, and frequently asked questions. If the chatbot cannot understand or answer a user’s inquiry, it will provide a default fallback response advising the user to contact the administrator or gym personnel for further assistance. The proposed system is intended for selected local fitness centers in Mamburao, Occidental Mindoro, specifically G-Fitness, Fitness Regency, and Ferrer Fitness. | Scope

Limitation
A few boundaries have been established to maintain the feasibility and attainability of the project within the academic time frame. The proposed CORE Fitness system focuses only on gym management, attendance monitoring, membership management, payment tracking, rule-based monitoring and analytics, and administrative support functionalities. The system does not provide nutritional planning, meal recommendations, medical health assessments, or automated fitness coaching services. Any workout recommendations, performance evaluations, and workout plans available in the system are manually provided by trainers and are not automatically generated by the system. Conceptual Framework
The study utilizes the Input-Process-Output (IPO) model as the basis for the conceptual framework of the proposed system. The IPO model is a commonly used framework in system development and research that illustrates the relationship between the inputs, process, and outputs of a system. It provides a clear visualization of how data and resources are transformed through a series of processes to produce a desired system output.

In this study, the inputs consist of the data, technologies, and resources required for the development of the system. The process includes the software development methodologies, system integration, implementation of system features, rule-based monitoring and analytics, and NLP-based administrative support functionalities. The output represents the developed CORE Fitness platform and its corresponding functionalities designed to improve gym operations, membership management, attendance monitoring, operational reporting, and administrative support services.

The gathered information serves as the analytical foundation for identifying systemic inefficiencies in manual gym operations and determining the necessary system features, functionalities, and intelligent technologies required for development. Deployment
The deployment stage transfers the fully verified software applications into a self-sustaining live operational environment. To achieve isolated system stability, a decentralized architecture separation of concerns strategy is enforced. The client-side admin React.js code layouts are distributed via edge routing mechanisms to minimize interface asset delivery latencies. Concurrently, the heavy server infrastructure components the computational Express.js API middleware instance, background analytics loops, NLP conversational text tokens parsing routines, and the underlying MySQL 8.0 database management system are hosted on an isolated, cloud-managed Virtual Private Server (VPS). Infrastructure Management: The CORE Fitness system utilizes an open-source technology stack composed of Node.js, MySQL 8.0, React.js, and React Native to minimize software licensing costs and support long-term system sustainability. The web-based administration dashboard and mobile application are designed to provide continuous access for administrators, trainers, and gym members.
Administrative Training: User competency and proper system utilization contribute to the sustainability of the proposed system. Orientation sessions and training activities will be conducted to guide administrators and trainers in using the web dashboard, attendance monitoring, membership management, payment tracking, trainer management, progress monitoring, booking management, and operational monitoring features of the system. 
Data Integrity and Security: To maintain system reliability and data consistency, regular database maintenance, security updates, and backup procedures will be implemented. MySQL 8.0 supports relational data integrity for attendance records, membership information, payment records, trainer records, progress monitoring data, ratings, feedback records, and other operational data to help prevent data loss and inconsistencies. 
Operational Cost Recovery: Local fitness centers may implement minimal technology or maintenance fees as part of gym membership services to support the maintenance and continuous operation of the digital management system as the local fitness community continues to grow.

Estimated Budget Requirements for Deployment 
To prevent unexpected costs, the system will minimize serverless function usage and enable caching for static pages and public content. 


Server Specifications for Front-end 
The CORE Fitness system utilizes Vercel as the primary hosting platform for the web-based front-end interface. The platform is responsible for delivering the React.js administration dashboard and handling front-end deployment and optimization services. The selected server configuration supports the operational requirements of the system, including user access, real-time interface rendering, and web application responsiveness. | Sustainability Plan

Expected Output

Centralized Gym Management Portal: A centralized web-based management portal for administrators to manage memberships, monitor attendance through QR code scanning, track payment records, manage schedules, and access operational reports in real time.
Rule-Based Monitoring and Analytics Dashboard: A monitoring and analytics dashboard that provides attendance summaries, membership activity reports, payment monitoring, renewal monitoring, attendance drop detection, inactive member monitoring, expired membership monitoring, operational reports, and data visualizations to support gym management and decision-making. The dashboard applies predefined system rules, such as flagging members with no recorded attendance for fourteen (14) consecutive days as inactive and identifying memberships nearing expiration for renewal reminders.
Member and Trainer Mobile Application: A cross-platform mobile application for Android and iOS devices that provides secure QR code-based identification, attendance monitoring, membership status viewing, account management, announcements, notifications, personal progress monitoring, trainer feedback viewing, trainer rating submission, goal tracking, and achievement badge monitoring.
Trainer Management and Recommendation System: A dedicated trainer module that enables trainers to manage schedules, review bookings, provide workout recommendations, submit performance evaluations, assign workout plans, and monitor member progress.
Centralized Back-End API and Database System: A centralized back-end system developed using Node.js, Express.js, and MySQL 8.0 that supports secure data processing, real-time data synchronization, database management, and communication between the web dashboard and mobile application.
ISO/IEC 25010 Evaluation Results: A comprehensive system evaluation report based on the ISO/IEC 25010 software quality standards, covering Functional Suitability, Reliability, Performance Efficiency, Usability, Security, Compatibility, Maintainability, and Portability.
User Acceptance Testing (UAT) Results: A user evaluation report containing feedback and assessment results from administrators, trainers, and gym members regarding the effectiveness, usability, and overall performance of the CORE Fitness system. | Expected Output
