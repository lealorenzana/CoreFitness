# The AI looks things up and says where (RAG), plus the coach's question card

Status: BUILT 2026-11-27 (steps 1 to 4 and 6 code); migrations not pasted, functions and apps not deployed; step 5 (legal line) waits for the owner. ROADMAP has the entry.

## Decisions (owner, 2026-11-27)
- Two assistants: the **member Assistant** (exists, `ai-coach`) learns to search documents; a new
  **owner and desk Help** assistant in admin Help.
- Search: **hybrid**, keyword AND meaning (owner, 2026-11-27: "then include it" after hearing that words alone
  miss "stop my membership" → freeze). Postgres full text search plus **pgvector** (0.8.2, available on the
  project, not yet enabled) with Supabase's built-in **`gte-small`** model (384 numbers per text, runs inside the
  Edge Functions, free, no outside API). The two rankings are merged by reciprocal rank fusion (Supabase's
  hybrid search pattern). If making the question's embedding fails, keyword search still answers.
- Answers **cite the source and link to it** (for example "Terms §5, Refunds" opening `/terms#refunds`).
- Help assistant draws from the **gym's AI credits**, with its own daily limit per staff account.
- Core Fitness knowledge, nothing sensitive: how to use the app (the 14 guides and what each screen does),
  what Core Fitness is and how to reach support (from the platform's own settings), the member Terms and
  Privacy (the gym's numbers filled in), and Core Fitness's plans and prices (owner Help only, from the price list).
- The coach's message limit: **ask all at once**. When the coach needs several details it sends one question
  card; the member fills it in and sends once, one message instead of one per question.

## What exists (read 2026-11-27)
- `ai-coach` already retrieves by tool: `get_gym_info` (0177, 0184, 0270) returns prices, hours, plans,
  house rules, the timetable, coaches and `how_this_gym_runs`. Its blind spots: the member Terms
  (`g-fitness-member/src/pages/Terms.tsx`, about 2,500 words) and Privacy (about 2,200), the waiver,
  announcements, and the guides (`lib/guides.ts`, about 4,500 words). About 10,000 words in all.
- Daily limit: `gym_settings.ai_daily_messages` (default 30, 0143); the chat counts every coach reply.
- Admin has no assistant.

## Acceptance criteria
Testable:
1. Member asks "can I freeze and for how long?": the coach calls the search, answers from the gym's own
   Terms and settings, and the answer carries a source chip that opens that clause.
2. A question the documents do not cover gets "I do not know, ask the front desk", never a guess.
3. A member's search never returns another gym's waiver, announcements or numbers (RLS and definer checks;
   red team suite stays green).
4. Owner asks "how do I set up time slots?" in admin Help: the answer names the page and the button from
   the guide, with a link to that page. The desk gets the same, minus anything the desk cannot open.
5. Help uses the gym's AI credits and stops at its own daily limit per staff account, saying so.
6. The coach needing goal, days and equipment sends one question card; submitting it costs one message.
7. When a guide or the Terms change, a check fails until the searchable copy is regenerated.

Needs the owner's judgment: whether the answers read well and cite the right clause.

## Design
**Library (migration 0282).**
- `knowledge_chunks (id, source, section, title, body, audience, link, tsv, embedding vector(384), body_hash)`: the shared texts, one row per
  clause or guide step. `source` = terms, privacy, guide, core_fitness, platform_plans; `audience` = member,
  staff, owner. Placeholders such as `{refund_fee}` are filled per gym when read, like `fillDocs()` (0262).
  RLS on, no policy; read only through the search function.
- Filled by a script, `scripts/knowledge-seed.mts`, from `Terms.tsx`, `Privacy.tsx` and `lib/guides.ts`,
  writing a seed migration; a CI check reruns it and fails on any difference (criterion 7).
- Gym texts are searched where they live: the current waiver, house rules and live announcements. Their
  embeddings sit in `knowledge_gym_vectors (gym_id, kind, ref_id, body_hash, embedding)`, refreshed when the
  text's hash changes.
- Embeddings are made only inside Edge Functions (the same `gte-small` for stored text and questions, never
  mixed with another model). `knowledge-embed` (service role only) fills any chunk or gym text whose hash
  changed; the assistants call it before searching, a few rows at a time, so nothing waits on a cron.
- `search_knowledge(p_query text, p_embedding vector(384), p_audience text)`: definer. Keyword rank
  (`websearch_to_tsquery('english', …)`) and meaning rank (cosine distance) over the shared chunks plus the
  caller's own gym texts, merged by reciprocal rank fusion; returns the top 6 with title, text and link.
  `p_embedding` may be null (keyword only). Revoked
  from public; granted to authenticated; audience checked against the caller's role in their gym.

**Member Assistant.** A new tool `search_gym_documents` calling `search_knowledge` as the member. The
system prompt: search before answering any rule, policy or how-to question; answer only from what comes
back; cite each source with its link. The chat renders the citations as small chips that open the page.

**Question card.** A new tool `ask_member` (strict schema: up to 8 fields, each a short label, a type
of choice, number or text, and options). The chat renders a card; Send submits every answer as one message.
The prompt: when you need more than one detail, ask them all in one `ask_member` call.

**Owner and desk Help.** A new Edge Function `staff-help` (same shape as `ai-coach`: the caller's JWT
for every gate, the service key only to record usage). Gate: admin or staff of the current gym, AI credits
left, its own `gym_settings.ai_staff_daily_messages` (default 20, an owner setting). Tools:
`search_knowledge` with audience owner or staff, and `get_gym_info`. A Help tab in the admin Help page.
